Mandarin Tone Recorder

Welcome to our experiment. You will be asked to read some Chinese syllables in Pinyin, which is a way to write Chinese using the Roman alphabet. Please fill out the information below. If you do not know any Chinese, that is fine, but please choose a speaker type of "other" and set the experiment condition to "tone-unspecified: unaccented prompt."

When you start, you will see a Pinyin syllable. Read it and click Next. If you wait too long, the recording will time out. If you make a mistake, click Redo instead of Next to record the syllable again. You may stop at any time by clicking Stop Session. You may also continue recording after the target session time has been reached.

We appreciate your effort, but participation in this experiment is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time.
