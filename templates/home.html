Mandarin Pronunciation Study

Record yourself reading Pinyin syllables for a language technology experiment. If you would like to record without specifying any contact information, click on the "Contribute recordings" button. If you are interested in using the technology developed from your data to improve your Mandarin pronunciation, please log in or sign up.

You will be asked for your language background information and your voice will be recorded and used to build speech recognition models that can help Manarin language learners. You will be able to choose how your speech is used. One option is to restrict the recordings to researchers at the University of St. Thomas and immediate collaborators. Another option is to share your recordings more broadly through a gated (non-public) repository (HuggingFace) with researchers for educational purposes only. Hugging face is a model and data repository that promotes the use of shared data and models.

No identifying information besides voice will be shared and only self-hosted speech recognition tools will be used (i.e., your speech will not be processed by third party services, only local running software and models).
